**Knowledge Check Updates**

**Detachment Question 5**

A facilitator strongly prefers an AI-generated solution but recognises
that the group needs more information before making a decision. Which
response best demonstrates Detachment?

A. Persuade the group to accept the solution because it appears to be
the strongest\
B. Withdraw from the discussion to avoid influencing the group\
C. Present the solution as one option, invite scrutiny and remain
willing to revise or release it\
D. Reject the solution to prove that the facilitator is not attached to
it

**Correct answer: C**

**Feedback:** Detachment does not require withdrawing from
responsibility or rejecting a preferred solution. It means remaining
engaged while allowing evidence, participation and the needs of the
situation to determine what happens next.

**Sense of Wonder** **Question 5**

**What is the best way to prevent "premature completion" when using
AI?**

A. Ask AI to produce a more detailed response\
B. Compare the output with answers from other AI tools\
C. Pause to examine the problem, question its assumptions and imagine
alternatives before accepting an answer\
D. Continue prompting until the output sounds convincing

**Correct answer: C**

**Feedback:** Premature completion occurs when a polished AI response
creates the impression that the thinking is finished. Sense of Wonder
keeps possibility open by encouraging us to examine the situation,
question the existing frame and consider what may still be missing.

**Intentionality** **Question 5**

**A team continues using an AI-generated plan even after new evidence
shows that it may not achieve the intended outcome. Which response best
demonstrates balanced Intentionality?**

A. Continue with the plan because changing direction would suggest that
the original decision was wrong.\
B. Abandon the project and avoid using AI for similar work in the
future.\
C. Revisit the purpose, assess the new evidence and adjust the plan
while remaining committed to the desired outcome.\
D. Ask AI to decide whether the team should continue or change
direction.

**Correct answer: C**

Balanced Intentionality maintains a clear sense of purpose while
remaining responsive to evidence, feedback and changing conditions.
