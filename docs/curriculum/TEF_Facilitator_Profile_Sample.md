**Feedback and recommendation**

**Current strengths**

Your strongest current patterns appear to be **Engagement**, **Sense of
Wonder**, and **Action**. You seem able to stay connected to people,
remain open to possibility, and move toward action when a response is
needed.

**Development priorities**

Your most important development opportunities appear to be
**Intentionality**, **Presence**, and **Focus**. These suggest that you
may benefit from strengthening your ability to clarify purpose, stay
steady in uncertainty, and keep responses aligned with what the
situation actually requires

**Possible overused tendencies**

-   Engagement may sometimes become over-identification.

-   Sense of Wonder may sometimes become uncritical openness.

-   Action may sometimes become premature intervention.

Your responses suggest that these tendencies may become more visible
when you are under pressure, strongly invested in an outcome, or trying
to move a situation forward quickly

**Recommended starting point**

**Start with Intentionality.**\
Your responses suggest that clarifying what you are trying to
accomplish, what responsibility must remain yours, and what should not
be delegated to the moment or to AI may be the most useful foundation
for improvement. Strengthening this discipline may also help you bring
greater coherence to Focus, Presence, and Action
